# 🎓 AI Career & Major Advisor

An AI-powered educational chatbot designed to help students explore suitable academic majors and build a clearer career development path.

## 🤖 About the Project

Choosing a university major can be challenging, especially when students are unsure about their interests, skills, and future career options.

This project was created as an early exploration of how conversational AI can support students in making more informed academic and career decisions.

## ✨ What It Does

- 🎯 Helps students explore suitable academic majors
- 💬 Provides an interactive conversational experience
- 🧭 Supports academic and career decision-making
- 📈 Suggests a clearer direction for personal and professional development

## 🛠️ Built With

- Artificial Intelligence
- Conversational AI
- Edcafe AI
- Prompt Design
